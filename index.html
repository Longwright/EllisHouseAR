Layers
  1. Stand on the bare slab of the floor you opened; move the phone until it finds the floor.
  2. The red pole marks the outer SW corner of the SW column (same column on every floor).
  3. Drag / twist until the pole sits on that corner, the red bar along the column's S face (east) and the blue bar along its W face (north).
  4. Expect 10–30 cm drift; re-align on each floor.

The 3D view opens standing on the stair landing (top of the stair from below; basement: foot of the stair); drag to look around, scroll / pinch out to leave. "Layers" hides / shows the model's collections as in Blender (house per floor: concrete, masonry, ETICS, openings, ceilings, stairs, floor fill; MVHR and fan coils per floor and part type); to look in from above, hide the floor above (e.g. on Ground: House › F1). The floor fill is hidden at first, so on site the model stands on the bare slab. Your choice is kept when you change floor and also applies in AR. "Free size in AR" lets you pinch to shrink / enlarge the model; leave it off on site for the true 1:1 model. AR needs Chrome on Android with "Google Play Services for AR" installed (Play Store); open this page in Chrome itself, not inside another app's browser.
